What I Do

The Kind House helps thoughtful businesses tell their story through words that feel honest and true to who they are.

Writing has been at the heart of my work for more than eight years.

I've been lucky enough to work with businesses across hospitality, wellness, lifestyle, sustainability, publishing, and the creative industries, helping them find a voice that feels true to who they are.

Along the way, I realised something important: people don't connect with businesses because everything looks perfect. They connect because something feels honest.

Over time, I realised words were never just one part of the job—they were the whole job. That's why The Kind House is a copywriting studio, and only a copywriting studio: no add-ons, no half-measures, just the words done properly.

About

Hi, I'm Amelia. I'm an Australian copywriter and editor. Writing has always been at the heart of what I do, and The Kind House grew from a love of helping thoughtful businesses find the right words to tell their story.

For me, words come first, and words come last too. A clever headline might catch someone's attention, but it's the right words all the way through that create connection. That's why I write every piece of copy myself, taking the time to understand what makes each business unique.

I keep The Kind House intentionally small, taking on just a handful of projects at a time. It gives me the space to get to know the people behind each business and create work that feels genuine, personal, and true to them.
