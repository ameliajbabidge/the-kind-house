What I Do

The Kind House helps thoughtful businesses find the right words—from website copy to articles, blogs, and brand messaging—and, when needed, designs websites that feel like home.

Writing has been at the heart of my work for more than eight years.

I've partnered with thoughtful businesses across hospitality, wellness, lifestyle, sustainability, publishing, and the creative industries, creating copy that feels clear, thoughtful, and genuinely human.

As my work evolved, I saw the same challenge appear time and again: businesses didn't just need better words—they needed websites that reflected the same level of care.

Today, The Kind House offers thoughtful copywriting and intentional website design, whether you need one or both.

About

Hi, I'm Amelia. I'm an Australian copywriter, editor, and creative director. Writing has always been at the heart of what I do, and The Kind House grew from a love of helping thoughtful businesses find the right words to tell their story.

For me, words come first. A beautiful website might catch someone's attention, but it's the right words that create connection. That's why I write every piece of copy myself, taking the time to understand what makes each business unique. Once the words are in place, I work closely with my small design team to bring them to life through thoughtful, beautifully crafted websites.

I keep The Kind House intentionally small, taking on just a handful of projects at a time. It gives us the space to get to know the people behind each business and create work that feels genuine, personal, and true to them.
